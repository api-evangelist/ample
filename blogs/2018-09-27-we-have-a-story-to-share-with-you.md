Last year, as we phased out our vegetarian product (Ample X) and started up Ample V, we faced the issue of food waste for the first time. We're not able to resell Ample that fails to deliver and returns to us unopened - nor can we sell Ample too close to the end of its shelf life, even though it's perfectly good and safe to eat. As a startup, this was not only a dilemma but a lesson in how much growth we have ahead of us to become more sustainable and prevent waste.
