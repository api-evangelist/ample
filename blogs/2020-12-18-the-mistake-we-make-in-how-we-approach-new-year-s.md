It would be totally understandable if setting New Year’s resolutions has entirely fallen off your radar after a crazy 2020. After all, getting through this year has been hard enough. And if you read anything about New Year’s resolutions, nearly every blog will show you dismal stats of resolutions success rates, followed by 7-10 tips to overcome the odds and be one of those in the successful camp.
